Our story

Cypress Customs started with one lift, one toolbox and a rule: do it right or don’t do it. Andrew Capmany opened the doors in East Los Santos, and word spread from one low car on one slow Sunday cruise to the next.

Today the shop is run by Andrew with Charles Broderick and Kevin Pousa on the floor. The cars changed, the rule didn’t.

“Do it right or don’t do it.”

Questions

How long does a job take?

Basic repairs are done while you wait. Builds and paint take longer, and we’ll tell you a time when we quote.

What cars do you work on?

Everything from daily drivers to full lowriders. Classics are our favourite.

Can you make a part that doesn’t exist?

Yes. Tell us what you want and which car it’s for, and we’ll build it to order. We quote the piece before we start.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer in game, paid when the work is finished.

Can you come to me?

Yes. Call the shop and our tow driver will bring your car in.
